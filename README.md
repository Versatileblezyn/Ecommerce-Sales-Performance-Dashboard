# Ecommerce Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes eCommerce sales performance using Microsoft Excel to provide insights into revenue, profitability, product performance, and regional sales trends.

The goal was to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that supports business decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Business stakeholders often struggle to quickly identify:

- Revenue performance over time
- Most profitable products
- Top-performing product categories
- Strongest sales regions
- Overall business profitability

This dashboard was developed to provide a centralized view of key business metrics and performance indicators.

---

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Dashboard Design

## Key Insights

### Revenue Trend

Revenue fluctuated throughout the year, with October recording the highest sales performance while November experienced the lowest revenue level.

### Top Products

Pen Set generated the highest revenue contribution, followed by Paper Pack and Cabinet.

### Product Categories

Office Supplies generated the highest sales contribution among all product categories.

### Regional Performance

West and North regions delivered the strongest profitability performance across the business.
